How to use this demo
Wallet links
Browsers can't open openid-credential-offer:// or openid4vp:// links. Scan the QR code with your wallet, or paste the link into it. To open them with a click on macOS or Linux, run eudi wallet register.
Credential offers
Your wallet downloads the offer from Keycloak, so it needs to reach Keycloak. An offer works once.
