eudi-dev 3.0.0 beta: a registrar for the test wallet

Version 3.0.0 adds a relying party registrar. Register your verifier or issuer, get its certificates and let the wallet check them the way the ARF describes.

  • Register verifiers and issuers in the web UI, with the CLI or over the TS05 registry API.
  • Access certificates (ETSI TS 119 411-8) for your own key, so your service can authenticate to the wallet.
  • Registration certificates (ETSI TS 119 475) for verifier_info and issuer_info. The registrar can revoke them.
  • ARF checks. With --arf the wallet checks both certificates and whether a verifier asks for more than it registered.
  • Attestation catalogue. Attestation types with their schemas and rulebooks (EC TS11).

Try the beta at preview.eudi-test.dev. The changelog lists everything new. Please report problems on GitHub.
