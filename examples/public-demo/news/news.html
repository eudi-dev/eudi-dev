What's new in eudi-dev v3

RegistrarRegister your verifier or issuer under Registrar. You get an access certificate and a registration certificate.
ARF checksThe wallet checks both certificates. It tells you when a verifier asks for more data than it registered.
Trusted listsAdd the CA of your issuer to a trusted list of the wallet, or add a list of your own.
Attestation catalogueLook up each credential type with its schemas, rulebook and trusted list.
