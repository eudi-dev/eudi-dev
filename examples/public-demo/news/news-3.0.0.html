What's new in eudi-dev 3.0

Test the trust side of the EUDI Wallet ecosystem. Register your verifier or issuer, get real certificates and let the wallet check them the way the ARF describes.

  • Test your verifier. Register it under Registrar and sign your requests with its certificates. The wallet tells you when you ask for more than you registered.
  • Test your issuer. Sign your metadata and publish your registration certificate. The wallet checks both before it accepts a credential.
  • See why a flow failed. The consent dialog lists every failed check.
  • Bring your own trust. Put your issuer's CA on one of the wallet's trusted lists.
  • Browse attestation types in the catalogue.
