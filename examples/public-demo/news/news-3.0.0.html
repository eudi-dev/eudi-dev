What's new in eudi-dev 3.0

Register your verifier or issuer, get real certificates and let the wallet check them the way the ARF describes.

Test your verifier or issuerRegister it under Registrar. Sign your requests or metadata with its certificates. The wallet tells you when you ask for more than you registered.
See why a flow failedThe consent dialog lists every failed check of the verifier or issuer.
Bring your own trustPut your issuer's CA on one of the wallet's trusted lists, or add a list of your own.
Browse attestation typesThe catalogue lists each credential type with its schemas, rulebook and trusted list.

Take the tour to see the new features in the wallet. What's new in 3.0 explains each feature. Please report problems on GitHub.
